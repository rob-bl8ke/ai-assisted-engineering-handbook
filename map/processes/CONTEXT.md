# Processes — CONTEXT

The three repeating verbs that move work through this repo.

## Inputs

- Object cards in `map/objects/` (processes consume and produce the nouns described there)
- `map/_meta/schema.md` — frontmatter requirements

## The three processes

| Process | What it does |
|---|---|
| [deliver](deliver.md) | Take a problem through the full software delivery lifecycle |
| [author](author.md) | Add, update, or validate knowledge in the handbook |
| [skill-ify](skill-ify.md) | Promote a mature Playbook to an executable Skill |

## Human check

Every process has at least one human gate. Verify that the gate is marked in the source before marking a process card `status: verified`.
